H1's banned question, H5's forbidden-offer list and H6's pre-verification word list were not newly reintroduced into the dialogue prompts by this diff. The principal new H4-style regression is runtime:666. Post-success “Your promise … is recorded” remains appropriate only after a true write success. Opening text required verbatim by a new bank should not be rewritten merely to avoid a token: honor the brief, and distinguish a call-recording disclosure from an unsupported promise-success claim.

The census/gap packages are intentionally policy material, not dialogue prompts. Their quoted rule descriptions must not be passed wholesale into a live instruction as a substitute for supported deterministic behavior.
